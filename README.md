# OffScripter 

**Think. Speak. Improve.**

OffScripter is an AI-powered technical communication practice platform that helps developers and students improve their ability to explain technical concepts clearly and confidently.

Knowing a concept is one thing. Explaining it under pressure is another.

OffScripter helps you practise spontaneous technical speaking through random topics, focused research sessions, voice recordings, and AI-powered feedback.

**Website:** https://offscripter.in

---

## Features

- **Random Topic Generation** – Explore 200+ technical topics across multiple categories.
- **Focused Research Timer** – Prepare your thoughts before speaking.
- **Voice Recording** – Record your explanation directly in the browser.
- **Speech Transcription** – Convert your recording into text.
- **AI-Powered Evaluation** – Receive structured feedback on your technical explanation and communication.
- **Practice History** – Review your previous attempts and track your progress.
- **Streak Tracking** – Build a consistent speaking-practice habit.
- **User Authentication** – Secure account management.
- **Pro Subscription** – Access premium practice features.


## Who Is OffScripter For?

- Students preparing for technical interviews.
- Developers who struggle to explain technical concepts.
- Engineers looking to improve their communication skills.
- Anyone who wants to become more confident speaking spontaneously about technology.

## Why OffScripter?

Technical knowledge alone isn't enough. Being able to communicate that knowledge clearly matters too.

OffScripter provides a structured way to practise explaining what you know, identify weaknesses, and improve with every attempt.
